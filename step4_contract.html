RISK WARNING / NOTICE TO PROSPECTIVE FRANCHISEES

A commercial franchise is a business investment. A franchise agreement is a legally binding contract that may pose significant financial risks.

Before you sign a franchise agreement or pay any money, you should carefully read this Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD). You are strongly advised to seek independent legal and financial advice.

Under applicable Canadian provincial franchise legislation, Y2C Holdings Inc. must provide you with this document at least 14 days before you sign any contract or pay any money to the franchisor or its affiliates.

Item 1. The Franchisor

The Franchisor is Y2C Holdings Inc., a corporation established under the laws of the Republic of Korea ("Franchisor", "we", "us", or "our"). We operate directly from South Korea without a local Canadian subsidiary or branch. We have been granted the Master Franchise Rights by Sinjeon Food Sys (the original trademark owner in Korea) to establish, operate, and grant sub-franchises for "Sinjeon Tteok-bokki" restaurants in Canada.

Item 2. Business Experience of Directors and Officers

Yoon-ji Lee, Director: Ms. Lee oversees global franchise expansion and operations. She brings extensive professional experience in global Supply Chain Management (SCM) and North American business operations. Her expertise ensures that overseas franchisees receive robust logistical support, optimal ingredient sourcing, and localized operational strategies.

Item 3. Litigation and Bankruptcy

Neither Y2C Holdings Inc. nor any of its directors or officers are currently involved in any material litigation, arbitration, or bankruptcy proceedings in Canada, the Republic of Korea, or any other jurisdiction.

Item 4. Initial Franchise Fee and Initial Investment

The Franchisee is required to pay the following initial fees directly to the Franchisor via international wire transfer.

[Gross-up / Net Payment Clause]: All payments must be made free and clear of, and without deduction or withholding for, any taxes, bank fees, or foreign exchange losses. If Canadian tax law requires any withholding (such as Part XIII tax), the Franchisee must gross up the payment so that the Franchisor receives the exact Net amount stated below.

  • Initial Franchise Fee: KRW 40,000,000 (Non-refundable except in the case of Franchisor's material breach).
  • Initial Education Fee: KRW 5,000,000 (Non-refundable).
  • Logistics Security Deposit: CAD $5,000 to $20,000 (Refundable). Depending on the designated regional supply chain structure, this deposit will be collected directly by the Franchisor or requested by an authorized local B2B logistics distributor.

Item 5. Ongoing Fees (Management Services)

Management & Support Fee: 4% of Gross Sales per month.
This fee is strictly designated as a Management Services Fee (Business Income) and not a trademark royalty. It compensates the Franchisor for remote professional services provided directly from South Korea.

Item 6. Territory

The Franchisee is granted an exclusive territory from the specific "Approved Location" designated in the Franchise Agreement. The Franchisor will not establish or grant a franchise to another party within this exclusive territory during the term of the agreement.

Item 7. Obligation to Purchase

To maintain the brand's uniformity and quality standards, the Franchisee must purchase designated proprietary ingredients and supplies exclusively from the Franchisor or suppliers approved by the Franchisor.

Item 8. Term, Renewal, and Dispute Resolution

  • Initial Term: 3 Years.
  • Renewal: The agreement does not automatically renew. A new agreement may be negotiated with a potential 20% discount on the new initial franchise fee.
  • Dispute Resolution & Governing Law: The Franchise Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Korea. All disputes shall be finally settled by binding arbitration in Seoul, Republic of Korea, in accordance with the International Arbitration Rules of the Korean Commercial Arbitration Board (KCAB).

  • Total Equity: KRW 91,847,896 | Net Income: KRW 5,914,229
